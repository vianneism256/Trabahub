================================================================================
  TRABAHUB — SOFTWARE WALKTHROUGH SCRIPT
  Estimated Duration: 16–18 minutes
  Presenters: VIANNE (Host / Admin), NASH (Freelancer), JUSTINE (Customer)
================================================================================

ROLES
  VIANNE  — Host, narrator, and admin panel demo
  NASH    — Freelancer account demo
  JUSTINE — Customer account demo

SETUP BEFORE STARTING
  - Vianne is logged in as admin on one browser tab
  - Nash has a freelancer account ready (KYC approved, 1 verified certification)
  - Justine has a customer account ready (KYC approved)
  - All three accounts have at least some connects in their balance
  - Open the app on a large screen so the audience can see clearly
  - Keep all tabs visible and organized before beginning

================================================================================
  SEGMENT 1 — INTRODUCTION (VIANNE)                               ~1.5 minutes
================================================================================

VIANNE:
  "Hi everyone. Welcome to Trabahub — a Philippine home services marketplace
  that connects customers who need skilled workers with verified freelancers
  in categories like plumbing, electrical, carpentry, cleaning, painting,
  HVAC, roofing, and landscaping.

  Today we're going to walk you through the full platform — how to sign up,
  get verified, post jobs, apply for jobs, and how our connect-based system
  works. I'll be demoing the admin side, Nash will show you the freelancer
  experience, and Justine will walk through the customer side."

[SCREEN: Show the landing page / login screen of Trabahub]
[Navigate to the Sign Up page by clicking "Sign Up" in the top right]


================================================================================
  SEGMENT 2 — SIGN UP FLOW (VIANNE)                               ~2 minutes
================================================================================

VIANNE:
  "Let's start with how a new user signs up. When you land on Trabahub,
  you'll see the Sign Up form. You fill in your first name, last name,
  email address, and password."

[SCREEN: Show the Sign Up page — /signup]
[Point to the First Name, Last Name, Email, and Password fields]

VIANNE:
  "Here you choose whether you're signing up as a Customer — someone looking
  to hire — or a Freelancer, someone offering services."

[SCREEN: Point to the 'I'm signing up as' dropdown showing Customer / Freelancer options]

VIANNE:
  "Before you can create your account, you need to read and agree to our
  Terms and Conditions. This checkbox must be checked — the button stays
  disabled until you agree."

[SCREEN: Point to the Terms and Conditions checkbox at the bottom of the form]
[Click the checkbox to show the 'Create Account' button become active]

VIANNE:
  "You can also sign in with Google using the button below the form.
  Once registered, you're taken directly to your dashboard."

[SCREEN: Point to the 'Sign in with Google' button]
[Do NOT actually create an account — just demonstrate the form visually]


================================================================================
  SEGMENT 3 — KYC VERIFICATION AND ADMIN APPROVAL (VIANNE)        ~3 minutes
================================================================================

VIANNE:
  "Now, new users can't post jobs or apply for jobs right away. Trabahub
  requires identity verification — what we call KYC, or Know Your Customer.
  Every user, both freelancers and customers, must upload a valid
  government-issued ID before they can use the platform fully."

[SCREEN: Navigate to Nash's freelancer account on a second tab or demo the Profile tab]
[Go to My Profile tab — click 'My Profile' in the top navigation]
[Scroll down to the Government ID section]

VIANNE:
  "When a user hasn't been verified yet, they see this prompt to upload their
  government ID — driver's license, passport, or national ID. Once uploaded,
  it goes to the admin for review."

[SCREEN: Show the dashed upload box and the 'Upload Government ID' button]

VIANNE:
  "Let me switch to the admin panel to show how that gets approved."

[SCREEN: Switch to Vianne's admin tab — navigate to /admin]
[The default tab should be 'KYC Verification' — if not, click it]

VIANNE:
  "Here in the admin dashboard, I can see all pending identity submissions.
  Each card shows the user's name, their role — whether they're a freelancer
  or a customer — their email, and a photo of their uploaded ID."

[SCREEN: Point to one of the KYC submission cards, showing the ID image and user info]

VIANNE:
  "I can click Review to open the decision panel, add a note if I need to,
  and either Verify or Reject their identity."

[SCREEN: Click the 'Review' button on a submission card]
[Show the Verify Identity and Reject buttons appearing]
[Click 'Verify Identity' to approve it]

VIANNE:
  "Once approved, the user gets a notification and their account unlocks.
  Freelancers can then submit certifications, and customers can post jobs."


================================================================================
  SEGMENT 4 — FREELANCER EXPERIENCE (NASH)                        ~5 minutes
================================================================================

NASH:
  "Thanks Vianne. So I'm logged in as a verified freelancer. Let me walk
  you through my dashboard."

[SCREEN: Switch to Nash's freelancer account]
[Navigate to 'My Profile' in the top navigation bar]

NASH:
  "This is my profile page. You can see my name, email, phone number,
  the specialties I've selected, and my overall rating and review count
  from past customers."

[SCREEN: Show the profile view page — scroll to show stats, name, bio, specialties]

NASH:
  "To get verified in a category, I go to Edit Profile and scroll down to
  the Certifications section."

[SCREEN: Click 'Edit Profile' button]
[Scroll down past the Government ID section to the Certifications section]

NASH:
  "I pick a category from the dropdown — say, Plumbing — then choose an
  image of my certification or license, and submit it for admin verification.
  Once the admin approves it, that category unlocks and I can see jobs in it."

[SCREEN: Show the certification dropdown and image upload button]
[Point to any existing verified certifications shown as green cards]

NASH:
  "Now let me show you the connect system. I'll click Buy Connects in the
  navigation."

[SCREEN: Click 'Buy Connects' in the top navigation tab bar]

NASH:
  "Connects are like bid tokens — every time I apply to a job it costs
  6 connects. To get connects, I buy a package and pay via GCash.
  We have four packages: Starter, Basic, Pro, and Power."

[SCREEN: Point to the four package cards showing connects and prices in pesos]
[Click the 'Basic' package card to select it]

NASH:
  "When I select a package, the GCash payment details appear — the number
  to send money to and the QR code I can scan. After I send the payment,
  I take a screenshot of the GCash confirmation and upload it here as my
  receipt."

[SCREEN: Show the GCash number, QR code, and the receipt upload area below]
[Click 'Choose Receipt Screenshot' to show the file picker — then cancel without uploading]

NASH:
  "Once I submit the receipt, admin reviews it and adds the connects to
  my balance — which you can see right here in the navigation bar."

[SCREEN: Point to the green '💰 X connects' pill in the top navigation bar]

NASH:
  "Now let me go to Browse Jobs."

[SCREEN: Click 'Browse Jobs' in the top navigation tab bar]

NASH:
  "This is the job feed. I can only see jobs in the categories I'm verified
  in. I can filter by a specific category using this dropdown. Each card
  shows the job title, description, budget, timeline, and the number of
  applications it already has."

[SCREEN: Show the jobs feed with the filter dropdown and job cards visible]
[Point to one job card — highlight the job title, budget, and categories]

NASH:
  "When I find one I like, I click Apply Now. It shows me it costs 6 connects.
  I write a short message to the client introducing myself, then hit Submit."

[SCREEN: Click 'Apply Now' on a job card — show the '— 6 Connects' label on the button]
[Show the message text area that appears]
[Do NOT submit — just show the form, then click Cancel]


================================================================================
  SEGMENT 5 — CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE (JUSTINE)                       ~4 minutes
================================================================================

JUSTINE:
  "Alright, my turn! I'm logged in as a verified customer. Let me show you
  what I can do on Trabahub."

[SCREEN: Switch to Justine's customer account]
[The default tab should be 'Find Freelancers' — if not, click it in the navigation]

JUSTINE:
  "The first thing I see is Find Freelancers. I can pick a category from this
  dropdown, and it loads all verified freelancers in that field."

[SCREEN: Click the 'Select a Category' dropdown and choose 'Plumbing']
[Wait for freelancer cards to load]

JUSTINE:
  "Each card shows the freelancer's name, their verified badge, star rating,
  their bio, and a button to View their full profile or contact them."

[SCREEN: Point to a freelancer card — highlight the verified badge, rating, categories]
[Click 'View Profile' on one card to show the profile modal]

JUSTINE:
  "I can see their full profile here — rating, number of reviews, jobs
  finished, bio, contact info. Great for picking the right person."

[SCREEN: Show the profile modal — scroll through it briefly]
[Click the X button to close the modal]

JUSTINE:
  "Now let me post a job. I'll click Post a Job in the navigation."

[SCREEN: Click 'Post a Job' in the top navigation tab bar]

JUSTINE:
  "Posting a job costs 4 connects. You can see my balance up in the nav bar.
  I fill in the job title, a description of what I need, an optional budget,
  and a timeline."

[SCREEN: Fill in the Job Title field with something like 'Fix leaking kitchen pipe']
[Fill in the Description field briefly]
[Point to the Budget and Timeline fields]

JUSTINE:
  "Then I select the categories I need — this determines which freelancers
  can see and apply to my job."

[SCREEN: Click one or two category checkboxes in the Required Skills section, e.g. Plumbing]

JUSTINE:
  "When I hit Post Job, 4 connects are deducted from my balance and the job
  goes live immediately. Freelancers with verified certifications in those
  categories will see it in their feed."

[SCREEN: Click the green 'Post Job' button]
[Show the success message that appears]

JUSTINE:
  "I can track all my jobs in My Jobs."

[SCREEN: Click 'My Jobs' in the top navigation tab bar]

JUSTINE:
  "Each job card shows whether it's Open or Closed, the date it was posted,
  the budget and timeline, and how many applications it has received.
  I can filter by open or closed, and sort by newest or oldest."

[SCREEN: Show the filter pills (All / Open / Closed) and the sort dropdown]
[Click 'Open' filter to show only open jobs]

JUSTINE:
  "When I click View Applicants, it takes me to the Messages tab where I
  can see all the freelancers who applied."

[SCREEN: Click 'View Applicants' on one of the job cards]
[The Messages tab opens showing conversation threads on the left panel]


================================================================================
  SEGMENT 6 — MESSAGING AND HIRING FLOW (NASH + JUSTINE)         ~2.5 minutes
================================================================================

JUSTINE:
  "This is the Messages tab. On the left, I can see each freelancer who
  applied, their name, the job they applied for, and the status of their
  application."

[SCREEN: Show the left panel of the Messages tab with conversation items]
[Click on one conversation to open the chat on the right]

JUSTINE:
  "I can chat with the freelancer here, share files or photos, and once I've
  decided, I click Assign Freelancer to hire them."

[SCREEN: Show the chat window and the 'Assign Freelancer' button in the header]
[Point to the message input box and the file attach button]

NASH:
  "And from my side, I can see the same conversation in my Messages tab.
  When the customer assigns me, my application status changes to Accepted
  and I get notified."

[SCREEN: Switch to Nash's account and show the Messages tab]
[Show the same conversation with the 'Accepted' status badge]

JUSTINE:
  "After the job is done, I can leave a star rating and written review for
  the freelancer directly from the conversation."

[SCREEN: Switch back to Justine's account, show the 'Leave a Review' button in the chat]
[Click it to show the star rating interface]
[Do NOT submit — just show the stars and text area, then click Cancel]


================================================================================
  SEGMENT 7 — NOTIFICATIONS AND CONNECT APPROVALS (VIANNE)        ~1.5 minutes
================================================================================

VIANNE:
  "Let me quickly show the notifications. Both freelancers and customers
  receive notifications for key events — KYC approvals, certification
  results, and connect purchase confirmations."

[SCREEN: Switch to Nash's freelancer account]
[Click 'Notifications' in the top navigation tab bar]

VIANNE:
  "Here Nash can see all notifications — when a certification is verified or
  rejected, when a job application is accepted or declined, and when a
  connect purchase is approved. Unread ones are highlighted in blue."

[SCREEN: Show the notifications list — point to unread (blue) vs read (white) items]

VIANNE:
  "On the admin side, when a connect purchase receipt is submitted, it shows
  up in the Connect Purchases tab with a red badge."

[SCREEN: Switch to Vianne's admin tab]
[Click the 'Connect Purchases' tab in the admin dashboard]

VIANNE:
  "Admin can see the receipt image, the package amount, and the price paid.
  One click to approve — and the connects are instantly added to that
  user's balance."

[SCREEN: Show a pending purchase card — point to the receipt image and approve button]


================================================================================
  SEGMENT 8 — CLOSING (VIANNE)                                    ~1 minute
================================================================================

VIANNE:
  "So that's Trabahub. To summarize what we covered:

  — Users sign up as either customers or freelancers
  — Everyone verifies their identity through our KYC system
  — Freelancers get certified in their trade categories by admin
  — Connects power the platform: 4 to post a job, 6 to apply
  — Customers find and hire verified professionals
  — Messaging, hiring, and reviews happen all in one place
  — Admin manages verifications and connect purchases in real time

  Trabahub is built to make hiring trusted home service professionals in
  the Philippines safe, simple, and transparent. Thank you!"

[SCREEN: Return to the landing page or the Trabahub logo / home screen]
[All three presenters visible if doing this live]

================================================================================
  END OF SCRIPT
  Total estimated time: 16–18 minutes
